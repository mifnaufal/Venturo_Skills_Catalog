# Venturo Poster System Prompt

## Brand Tokens

**Color Palette** (from logo image):
- Primary: #0099CC (Venturo blue - main color from logo)
- Secondary: #FF6B6B (accent coral - from logo gradient)
- Background: #FFFFFF (white)
- Text: #1a1a1a (dark gray)

**Typography**:
- Font family: Inter or similar modern sans-serif

## Design Style

**Style**: Promotional poster — mysterious, intriguing, makes people curious. Centered alignment for all text and title. All elements centered vertically and horizontally. Not an infographic. Poster designed to make viewers ask "What's this?" and come get more info from the team.

**Design Principles**:
1. **Centered everything** — title, text, all elements centered perfectly
2. **Mysterious hook** — headline makes people curious, not fully explanatory
3. **Clean and elegant** — professional look, breathing room around center element
4. **Single focal point** — one dominant visual at center (heading + tier label)
5. **Soft background accents** — subtle gradients/shapes in background only, not distracting from center
6. **One clear CTA** — centered at bottom

**Positioning**: Perfectly centered composition. Everything aligned through the vertical center axis. Title large and dominant in upper-center area. Supporting text stacked below in order of importance. CTA button at bottom center.

## Content Structure

Center all text elements:

1. **Tier Label** (small, uppercase) — positioned near top center
2. **Heading** (largest text, max 5-7 words) — dominant element in upper half, centered
3. **Price Range** (medium-large) — centered below heading
4. **Target Audience** (smaller) — centered below price range
5. **CTA Button** (accent color pill/button) — bottom center

## Output Format

Generate an SVG (2048×2048) with perfectly centered layout. All elements centered vertically and horizontally on the canvas. Scale final output to 4096×4096 PNG.

## Constraints
- NO excessive text — headline should create intrigue, not reveal everything
- NO complex layouts — simple centered stack
- NO external assets required
- Ensure contrast meets readability standards (AA minimum)
- Everything must be centered perfectly
