# Venturo Brand & Design Theme

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (Deep Teal) | `#006D79` | Headlines, primary CTA, key accents |
| Secondary (Light Teal) | `#009BAD` | Sub-headings, badges, secondary accents |
| Accent (Green) | `#10B981` | Success states, highlights, checkmarks |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | Main canvas / clean sections |
| Background Alt | `#F0FDFA` | Subtle tinted sections |
| Text Primary | `#0F172A` | Body text, labels |
| Text Muted | `#64748B` | Descriptions, captions |

## Style Direction

> Clean, modern, and confident Indonesian tech-professional aesthetic.
> Bold geometric shapes and diagonal accents create dynamism.
> Ample white space for readability.
> Phone/device mockup on one side, text content on the other.
> Floating badges and subtle dot-grid background texture.
> Gradient overlays using the brand teal-to-green spectrum.
> Rounded corners (8–12px) on cards and buttons.
> Soft drop shadows for depth without clutter.

## Typography Direction

> **Headline font:** Inter or Poppins — ExtraBold (800), uppercase, dominant size.
> **Key word emphasis:** italic on the focal word inside the headline (e.g., PAKET *STARTER*).
> **Body font:** Inter — Regular (400) for descriptions, Medium (500) for labels.
> **Button/CTA font:** Inter — SemiBold (600), uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em.
> **Hierarchy:** Headline > Subtitle/Tagline > Section label > Body > Caption.
> All text must be legible at thumbnail size (minimum 14px equivalent in 1:1 canvas).

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Keep theVenturo brand teal as the dominant color (>40% of visual weight)
- Use the green accent sparingly for highlights and success indicators
- Maintain clear visual hierarchy — headline must be the first thing seen
- Include a phone/mockup element on at least one side of the composition

**Don't:**
- Do NOT generate QR codes or QR-code-like grid patterns
- Do NOT use clashing colors outside the palette
- Do NOT crowd the layout — preserve white space
- Do NOT invent text that isn't provided in the context files
